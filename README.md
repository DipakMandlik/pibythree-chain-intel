# Pibythree Chain Intel

MASTER BUILD PROMPT

Pibythree — DMART Supply & Demand Intelligence

Build a complete, polished, enterprise-grade web application called:

Pibythree Supply & Demand Intelligence

Product tagline

Predict demand. Optimize inventory. Strengthen procurement.

This application is being created by Pibythree as an AI-powered retail supply-chain intelligence showcase for a DMART client demonstration.

The application must feel like a real enterprise product, not a prototype, generic analytics dashboard, or collection of static mockup pages.

The three business capabilities that must be integrated into ONE connected product are:

Predictive Demand Intelligence

Autonomous Inventory Intelligence

AI-Augmented Procurement

These three capabilities must work together as one continuous decision-intelligence workflow:

Demand → Forecast → Inventory Risk → Recommendation → Replenishment / Transfer → Procurement → Supplier Decision → Business Impact

The DMART source material describes the three areas around granular demand forecasting, seasonal/promotional/local demand signals, inventory positioning, stock-risk prediction, replenishment and transfer recommendations, supplier/pricing intelligence, RFQ acceleration and procurement savings. Preserve this business framing in the application.

1. MOST IMPORTANT PRODUCT OBJECTIVE

The application must communicate:

Pibythree transforms retail data into actionable supply-chain intelligence.

Do NOT build three disconnected dashboards.

The user should be able to start with a demand problem, move into inventory implications, and then move into procurement implications without feeling like they entered another application.

Example journey:

Demand forecast increases → inventory risk detected → AI identifies affected stores → AI recommends transfer → supplier delivery risk discovered → procurement recommendation generated → business impact calculated.

This connected experience is the central product concept.
